Manual seam-line picker

For reco-calibrate's fit_ground_tilt_manual harness (see crates/reco-calibrate/FRICTION.md point 18). Load the GPU-undistorted frame_XX_left.png / frame_XX_right.png images that reco calibrate --debug-dir <dir> ... already dumps. On each side, click two points along the same real field line, near the seam - one further from the camera, one closer - then "Add line pair". Repeat for at least 2 different lines (2 independent points are needed to fit ground_tilt_x and ground_tilt_z - 1 line falls back to a single shared parameter). Nothing here is uploaded anywhere - it's a plain static file, runs entirely in this tab.
